CORE ARCHITECTURE

The 4-Layer Screening Engine

Each document passes through four independent analytical modules before synthesizing a unified decision report.

01

OCR Extraction & MRZ Parsing

Automatically isolates Machine Readable Zones (MRZ) per ICAO 9303 specifications. Built-in OCR auto-correction repairs digit confusion ($O \rightarrow 0, I \rightarrow 1, Z \rightarrow 2$) in numeric blocks.

  • Passport, Visa, National ID & Licence support
  • Surname, Given Names, Passport No, DOB & Expiry
  • Keyword-based fallback for visual zone text
02

Algorithmic Checksum Validation

Applies strict modulo-10 weighted ($7, 3, 1$) mathematical verification over raw MRZ characters. Instantly catches printed text tampering or altered document numbers.

  • Passport Number check digit math
  • Date of Birth & Expiry sanity checks
  • Composite check digit overall integrity score
03

Image Forensics & ELA Tampering

Error Level Analysis (ELA) re-compresses document canvases to reveal compression variance. Highlighted heatmaps expose spliced photos, altered numbers, or fake stamps alongside EXIF metadata scans.

  • Pixel-level compression variance heatmap
  • Editing software signature detection (Photoshop/GIMP)
  • Localized anomaly & photo-swap alert
04

Biometric Face Verification

Runs on-device ResNet deep facial neural networks to extract 128-dimensional biometric embeddings from document photos and live webcam captures for instant distance matching.

  • 128-d Euclidean distance face similarity
  • WebRTC Live Camera webcam capture modal
  • Anti-impersonation match/mismatch verdict
